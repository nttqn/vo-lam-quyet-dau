**Mô tả đầy đủ (Full description)**

Bái sư nhập môn, luyện thành tuyệt kỹ, rồi một mình một kiếm bước vào giang hồ.

Võ Lâm Quyết Đấu là game đối kháng võ lâm 3D, đấu một chọi một. Bạn điều khiển nhân vật bằng cần gạt ảo và nút đòn: đấm, đá, né, đỡ, tung chiêu thức. Mỗi trận là một màn đọc đòn, phản đòn và chớp thời cơ ra tuyệt kỹ.

NGŨ PHÁI TRANH HÙNG
• Thiếu Lâm dùng côn, cương mãnh, chịu đòn tốt.
• Võ Đang dùng kiếm, lấy nhu chế cương, kiếm khí tầm xa.

ĐÁNH ĐẤM CÓ CHIỀU SÂU

• Y phục riêng cho từng phái.

TIỆN LỢI

## English

**Title**

Vo Lam Quyet Dau: Wuxia Duel

**Short description**

3D wuxia fighting game: pick a sect, master its martial arts and duel the world.

**Full description**

Join a sect, master its secret techniques, and walk into the martial world with nothing but your blade.

Vo Lam Quyet Dau is a one-on-one 3D wuxia fighting game. You fight with a virtual stick and action buttons: punch, kick, dodge, block and unleash techniques. Every fight is about reading your opponent, countering, and choosing the right moment for your ultimate.

FIVE SECTS
• Shaolin: staff, heavy blows, hard to knock down.
• Wudang: sword, softness over force, ranged sword energy.
• Emei: twin swords, fast chains.
• Beggars' Sect: palm strikes and drunken fist.
• Tang Clan: hidden weapons and flying knives that control the distance.
• A hidden sect opens only to those who prove themselves.

DEEP COMBAT
• Six techniques and one ultimate for every sect.
• Pressure points: hit the arms, legs or core to weaken blows, slow the legs or drain inner energy.
• Insight: block and dodge at the right moment to learn other sects' techniques.
• The finishing blow turns the screen into an ink-wash painting.

THE MARTIAL WORLD
• A story across 8 regions and 48 fights, plus a Nightmare mode for veterans.
• Trial Tower: endless floors, a twist rule every third floor and a guardian every tenth.
• Arena: the longer your win streak, the bigger the prize.
• Dungeons: farm materials for your forge.
• Duels: challenge other players' heroes and climb the weekly ranking.

FORGE AND OUTFITS
• Enhance gear up to +15; high-grade weapons glow.
• Raise rarity, reroll bonus lines, and study manuals to power up techniques.
• Outfits for every sect.

CONVENIENT
• Story, tower, arena and dungeons play offline.
• 3 character slots at the start; a 4th opens once a hero reaches level 30.
• Cloud save when you link an account.

Free to play, with ads. Rewarded ads are optional; from level 10 there are also occasional ads between screens (at least 4 minutes apart, never during a fight). Available in English, Tiếng Việt, 繁體中文 and 한국어; World Chat is split by language.
